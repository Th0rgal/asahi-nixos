## 3. MacBook Air M2 Hardware Polish (`modules/hardware-m2air.nix`)

Implemented and wired into `hosts/m2air.nix`:

- **Audio DSP & Speaker Safety:** `hardware.asahi.setupAsahiSound = true` (`asahi-audio 4.1` PipeWire + WirePlumber DSP graph with `bankstown-lv2`, `lsp-plugins`, `triforce-lv2`, `alsa-ucm-conf-asahi v10`, and mandatory `speakersafetyd 3.0.0` thermal protection) + `wiremix` / `pamixer` TUI/CLI controls.
- **Display, Fractional HiDPI & Notch Geometry:** `eDP-1` configured at `2560x1664@60` with `scale = "1.6"` (`1600x1040` logical), `xwayland.force_zero_scaling = true`, `hardware.asahi.avd.vaapi-support = true` (`asahi-va-driver` + `avd-fw`), `wf-recorder` for dual-DRM Apple Silicon screen capture, and `/usr/share/omarchy-platform/display-cutouts.json` (`300x64` camera notch cutout).
- **Power & Battery Longevity:** `schedutil` governor (Energy Model), `services.upower` (critical poweroff at 4%), `services.power-profiles-daemon`, Wi-Fi powersave with Apple Silicon `brcmfmac` firmware supplicant preserved, `zramSwap` (`zstd`, 50%), `asahi-bless` startup disk selector, and `macsmc-battery-thresholds.service` (`75%` start / `80%` end charge cap when `macsmc-battery` sysfs is writable).
- **Sleep / Wake (`s2idle`):** `SuspendState=freeze` (hibernation disabled on Asahi), lid-close `suspend` (ignored when docked), power-key tap ignored in `logind` so accidental Touch ID taps never shut down the session, `hyprlock` engaged before sleep via `hypridle`, and DPMS restored on resume.
